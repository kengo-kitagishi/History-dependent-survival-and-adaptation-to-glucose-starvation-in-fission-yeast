Introduction

## Stage 1: セクションタイトルレベル
 
### タイトル案
"General Introduction: Molecular crowding, intracellular fluidity, and the physical basis of history-dependent survival under glucose starvation"
 
### Highlights（thesis全体）
1. Intracellular macromolecular crowding and fluidity in fission yeast change dynamically during glucose starvation in a history-dependent manner, as revealed by QPM and GEM-based passive microrheology
2. The cytoplasmic physical state — quantified by dry mass density and intracellular diffusivity — predicts single-cell survival probability upon subsequent starvation
3. Entry into dormancy is associated with a crowding-driven fluid-to-solid-like cytoplasmic transition, the extent of which depends on prior starvation history
 
### Introduction構成
1. **General background (Communication purpose):** Cells in fluctuating nutrient environments must survive starvation; survival is heterogeneous even in clonal populations; history matters
2. **Technical background (Technical purpose):** Dormancy as the relevant survival state; cytoplasmic physical state (crowding, fluidity) as its physical determinant; LLPS as a mechanistic link
3. **Specific problem:** How starvation history shapes cytoplasmic physical state at single-cell resolution is unknown; the link to survival probability is not established
4. **Resolution:** QPM + GEM probes in S. pombe to establish the physical basis of history-dependent survival
